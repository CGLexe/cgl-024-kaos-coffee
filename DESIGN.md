## Brand & Style

This design system defines an ultra-clean, contemporary identity for a specialty coffee and bubble tea space situated in Oaxaca. The aesthetic bridges architectural precision with raw street-level energy: brutalist discipline meets playful sensory indulgence. 

The visual language balances stark negative space, editorial type-setting, and high-impact micro-accents. It evokes the feeling of premium tactile packaging—heavy unbleached paper, stark jet-black ink stampings, and vivid fluorescent tape seals. Designed for discerning urbanites, coffee obsessives, and modern travelers, the interface prioritizes immediacy, crisp optical hierarchy, and sudden bursts of irreverence (such as a recurring minimalist "mascot eyes" badge).

Key attributes:
- **Clinical Editorial Structure:** Grounded in grid rigor, uncluttered white space, and asymmetric layout cues.
- **Electric Tactility:** Sharp mechanical boundaries punctuated by intense, high-energy neon moments.
- **Controlled Playfulness:** Micro-interactions and characterful details that surprise without cluttering the ordering and reading flows.

## Colors

The palette operates on high-tension chromatic contrast. It sets deep obsidian darks and razor-sharp cobalt blue against an architectural off-white canvas, using acid lime purely as an kinetic focal point.

- **Primary (`#1565C0` - Electric Blue):** Structural authority, navigational hierarchy, active states, key CTAs, and digital link points.
- **Secondary (`#E6FF00` - Acid Lime):** High-voltage accentuation. Reserved exclusively for highlights, promotional callouts, active tags, and the playful mascot gaze. Never used for long-form text or subdued containers.
- **Tertiary (`#121212` - Deep Onyx):** Deep grounding neutral. Used for dense typography, structural dividers, and inverted surface treatments.
- **Neutral (`#F8F9FA` - Off-White) & Concrete (`#EDEDED`):** Canvas backgrounds, surface elevation tiers, subtle border demarcations, and negative space fields that evoke gallery-grade spaces.

Dark mode flips the visual hierarchy: the canvas descends into `#121212`, card surfaces shift to `#1E1E1E`, typography converts to `#F8F9FA`, while `#E6FF00` and `#1E88E5` provide luminous foreground contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a calibrated tripartite system:

1. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines & Display):** Selected for its geometric stance, tight aperture, and contemporary poise. Set tight with negative tracking to convey density and punch.
2. **Inter (Body & Narrative):** The systematic, highly legible workhorse for menu descriptions, origin stories, and drink customization menus.
3. **Space Grotesk (Labels, Badges, Metrics):** Introduced as the playful, technical accent. It delivers the packaging label feel—serving roast profiles, milk ratio indicators, bubble tea sweetness levels, and micro-metadata with a stylized, industrial touch.

Always set uppercase labels with extended tracking (`letterSpacing >= 0.04em`). Display headers must adhere to tight vertical line packaging without loose leading.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid for desktop and tablet screens, shifting to a 4-column layout on mobile devices. 

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`. Content is bounded by a maximum container width of `1360px` to maintain optical balance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`. Complex modular cards collapse into seamless, vertical swipe-stacks.

Spacing rules prioritize airy, intentional groupings over even distributions. Component internal padding relies on tight multiples (`space-sm`, `space-md`), whereas section-level separations demand dramatic breathing room (`space-xl` or double `space-xl`) to establish an editorial pace.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, multi-layered diffuse shadows in favor of a crisp tactile plane model. Visual stacking is achieved through:

1. **Surface Tone Layering:** Canvas base sits at `#F8F9FA`. Tier-1 elevated containers (menu tiles, customization panels) use pure `#FFFFFF`. Sub-recessed trays use `#EDEDED`.
2. **Crisp 1px Boundaries:** Containers employ hairline borders (`#121212` at 8% opacity in light mode, or pure `#121212` for primary focal blocks) to define strict architectural edges.
3. **Offset Hard Shadows (Tactile Lift):** Interactive cards, hover states, and sticker-style labels feature a zero-blur, high-contrast offset: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #121212`. This imparts a physical print/sticker quality.
4. **Frosted Translucency (Overlays & Nav):** Floating headers and checkout overlays use a high-density blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with 85% alpha background tinting (`rgba(248, 249, 250, 0.85)`), letting content scroll underneath like tracing paper.

## Shapes

The geometric tone balances industrial precision with subtle ergonomics:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Applied to cards, containers, input fields, and technical spec-sheets to maintain a crisp, machined look.
- **Large Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used on modals, bottom sheets, and secondary interactive wrappers.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Strictly restricted to specific micro-components: status chips, tags, quantity pills, and the "mascot eyes" badge container. This stark contrast between rigid boxes and full circular pills creates energetic visual friction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1565C0` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, font `Space Grotesk` (semi-bold, uppercase). On hover, shifts to `4px 4px 0px #121212` drop offset with `-2px -2px` transform.
- **Accent (Action/Order):** Solid `#E6FF00` fill, `#121212` text, with persistent `2px 2px 0px #121212` shadow. Instant tactile press depression (`0px 0px`) on active state.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** 1px border in `#121212` with transparent fill, filling with `#121212` and text inverting to `#FFFFFF` on hover.

### Mascot Eyes Badge
- A brand-defining graphic device. Two minimalist, horizontal black oval pupils set inside a pill-shaped neon yellow (`#E6FF00`) or white badge. Micro-animated on hover: pupils track cursor direction or execute a swift blink during state transitions.

### Cards & Menu Tiles
- Pure `#FFFFFF` background with a subtle `1px solid #EDEDED` perimeter. 
- Integrated image blocks use edge-to-edge bleed at the top with a desaturated, high-contrast photography treatment.
- Metadata sections feature `Space Grotesk` tags detailing origin (e.g., "PLUMA HIDALGO, OAXACA"), roast profile, or tea base.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Default:** `#EDEDED` background with `#121212` label text.
- **Active/Selected:** `#121212` background with `#E6FF00` text.
- **Specialty/Alert:** `#E6FF00` background with `#121212` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat `#FFFFFF` fields framed with a `1px solid #EDEDED` border, expanding to `2px solid #1565C0` on focus.
- Placeholder text in subdued charcoal; labels positioned above in uppercase `Space Grotesk` (`label-sm`).
- No internal drop shadows.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkboxes: Square with `2px` radius; checked state fills with `#1565C0` displaying a crisp white geometric check.
- Custom Steppers & Drink Modifiers (Ice level, Sugar percentage, Boba options): Segmented bar controls rendered in hairline-bordered pill tracks with sliding `#121212` highlight tabs.

### Lists & Accordions
- Clean horizontal rules (`1px solid #EDEDED`) dividing editorial line-items.
- Hovering over a list item exposes a quick-order button alongside a subtle micro-preview thumbnail.
